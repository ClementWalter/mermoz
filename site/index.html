Built for a lighter workflow

An inbox that behaves like a flight plan: everything has a status, a destination, and a key to get it there.

Sorted before you read it

Each new email arrives with a suggested status and project. Read it and the suggestion applies, or press y.

One key, done

e done, a archive, ⌫ delete, ⌘Z undo. Select several with ⇧ and move them together.

Flows, not folders

Unread, To do, Waiting, Done. A list or a board, grouped and ordered your way. Your Gmail labels are your projects.

Read what matters

Original, exactly as sent. Or Optimized: the same words without signatures, disclaimers and quoted history. Made once, kept.

Your whole mailbox, instant

Every message lives on your Mac. Search, read and sort offline, with ⌘K for anything else.

Agents draft, you approve

Scripts and AI agents work through a command line and MCP. Nothing they write leaves without your OK.

Why “Mermoz”

Named after a pilot who carried the mail.

In 1930, Jean Mermoz flew the Aéropostale mail across the South Atlantic, from Senegal to Brazil, in a little over twenty hours. Letters that used to take weeks by sea arrived in days.

The Aéropostale pilots had a motto: « Le courrier doit passer », the mail must go through. Mermoz keeps that spirit: mail that moves, light and fast, without the heavy machinery around it.
