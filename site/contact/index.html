Contact Mermoz

Mermoz is a small, independent project in private beta. The fastest way to reach us depends on what you need.

Email

Write to clement@mermoz.xyz for anything personal: beta access, an invitation that didn't arrive, a privacy question or a request to delete your sign-up address. Beta testers can also simply reply to their invitation email. Every message is read by a person.

Bugs and ideas

Open an issue on GitHub. Mermoz is open source, so issues, discussions and fixes are public. Please say which version you run (Mermoz › About Mermoz, or mermoz --version) and what you expected to happen. Never paste the content of private emails or an API key into a public issue.

Feedback from agents

If you drive Mermoz from an AI agent, the agent can report friction itself:

mermoz feedback "search ignores accents in subject" --command search --agent claude-code

The MCP server offers the same as the submit_feedback tool. Feedback is stored in your local mailbox and listed in Mermoz › Settings › Developer; share it with us by email or in an issue when you want us to see it.

Joining the beta

Leave the Gmail or Outlook address you'll use in the form on the homepage. Mermoz is in private beta while Google reviews its Gmail access, so each address is added by hand; you'll get an email when you can sign in.
